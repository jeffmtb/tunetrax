# TuneTrax headstock concepts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool using the supplied guitar photo as reference. These are visual concepts; controls shown in the images are not interactive.

## Steampunk

Use case: ui-mockup / style-transfer.
Create one portrait hand-drawn illustration concept for the TuneTrax guitar tuning app, using the attached guitar headstock photograph as the structural reference. This is a polished illustrated app background and control concept, no phone frame. Keep the recognizable straight-on six-string acoustic guitar headstock silhouette, three tuning keys on each side, six posts and six strings leading down to a short visible neck at bottom. Fill most of the portrait canvas with the headstock and leave comfortable margins around the six side keys. All six tuning keys must be fully visible, distinct, large and shaped as tactile clickable buttons, with clear note labels on the outward key buttons: left top D, left middle A, left bottom E₂; right top G, right middle B, right bottom E₄. Exactly six keys/buttons, no additional keys. The six front string posts remain illustrated mechanical details. Highlight the lower-left E₂ key as the selected string with one restrained colored halo. Replace the YAMAHA lettering at the crown with a clearly readable two-position horizontal toggle flanked by a microphone icon at left and a speaker icon at right; small text below the corresponding icons reads LISTEN and PLAY. The toggle controls the action performed when selecting a string button: listening for tuning or playing its reference tone. Show LISTEN active. Remove all Yamaha wordmarks and logos. Keep the UI simple and visually integrated into the drawing. A small TuneTrax wordmark may appear at the bottom. Show authentic pencil and ink contours, crosshatching and visible hand-painted paper texture, completely illustrated rather than photorealistic, no 3D render, no polished photographic reflections, no extra panels or menus. Portrait 2:3 composition.
Theme: STEAMPUNK. Warm sepia ink and muted watercolor on smoky cream paper; reddish walnut headstock with aged brass edging, copper strings, restrained small gear details around the six existing tuning mechanisms. Six brass key buttons, engraved note letters, tiny teal accent for the active E₂ key and LISTEN toggle. The top switch is an elegant brass instrument control. Beautiful mechanical field-sketch aesthetic, sparse background so controls read clearly.
